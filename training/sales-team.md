# I-World Admin Hub — Sales Team One-Pager

**Login:** admin site → sign in with your `@iworldnetworks.net` email + the password given by your supervisor. First time? Verify your email to unlock access.

## Your pages & what to use them for

### Sales Dashboard (`/admin/sales`)
Review region, agent, and segment performance at a glance. **Look at:** totals, active vs inactive, MRR/ARPU, segment table.

### Records (`/admin/sales/records`)
Your day-to-day data home.
- **Add record** (top of page) — enter a customer, plan, region, status. Validation will highlight anything wrong — fix the red fields.
- **Search** box to find a customer; **filters** for region/status.
- **Edit / Delete** per row (delete asks for confirmation).
- **Export CSV / PDF** top-right — hand exports to your supervisor for reports.

### Import Data (`/admin/sales/import`)
- Bulk-upload a spreadsheet of records. Preview the mapping first, then import.
- Each import gets a batch ID — if you import by mistake, that ID lets you find & remove the batch.

### Targets (`/admin/sales/targets`)
- Set monthly targets for agents/regions. Keep these current — KPI reports compare actual vs target.

### Monthly Revenue (`/admin/sales/monthly-revenue`)
- Monthly tables: revenue, MRR, new customers. Switch the metric to see different views.

### BTS Audit (`/admin/bts/audit`)
- **Add BTS Audit Record** — BTS name, address, status, region. The audit period auto-fills to the current week.
- Filter by status/region, search, edit, delete.

### BTS Data (`/admin/bts/import`)
- Import your customer site list per region; watch Import History to roll back a bad batch.

## Don'ts
- Never create accounts here — that's done by the super admin in the backend console.
- Don't delete another agent's records without confirming with them first.

## Your weekly routine
1. Monday: check Targets — are they current for the month?
2. Verify last import Batch in Records; reconcile totals.
3. Run the weekly Export PDF and send to your supervisor.
4. Add any new BTS audit records during the week.
