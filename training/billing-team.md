# I-World Admin Hub — Billing & Revenue One-Pager

**Login:** admin site → sign in with your `@iworldnetworks.net` email + password from the last supervisor contact. Verify email on first login.

## Billing (`/admin/billing`)
- Billing overview KPIs (totals, outstanding).
- **Billing Issues** list — every customer who submitted **Billing** feedback (e.g. wrong charge, late invoice, overage) appears here.
- On the issues list: click the record → set status → add resolution notes → Save. That marks it resolved in the dashboard reports.

## Support Revenue (`/admin/support-revenue`)
- Records the revenue from **support engagements/retainers**.
- **Add a record**: project type, amount, dates, notes.
- **Edit** after prices change or corrections.
- **Delete is only possible for Super Admin emails** — if you need to remove an entry, request it or edit instead of delete.

## Where data comes from
- Billing categories flow in from **customer feedback** (public form + Splynx webhook).
- Your resolution notes are visible to management in the Dashboard / PDF report.

## Don'ts
- Don't delete records unless the super admin asks — it affects the financial reports.
- Don't create customers here; customer data lives in records/sites imported by Sales.

## Daily routine
1. Open Billing → new issues? Work through them: resolve + note.
2. End of week: confirm Support Revenue records match your reconciliation.
3. Use the Dashboard PDF export for your monthly close-out report.
